### 🔭 About me

I study how seismic waves scatter in the heterogeneous crust, and how a hidden anomaly
can be told apart from the random clutter around it. My path into geophysics started
in **electrical engineering**: a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering & Automation, then a
year keeping the mission-critical power systems of a national government data center
running. That background in signals, hardware and fault diagnosis shapes how I approach
wave physics today.

- 🌊 **Now:** M.S. thesis, *Comparison of Full-Wave Seismic Scattering and Radiative Transfer Simulations in Random Media*
- ✍️ **Writing:** first-author manuscript on P–S wavefield redistribution and coda development caused by a localized heterogeneous anomaly
- 🎯 **Interests:** wave scattering · subsurface / GPR sensing · stochastic media · simulation-driven data for few-shot detection · signal analysis of power hardware and power grids

#### [seismic-model](https://github.com/mdsabbirhossain90/seismic-model)

A 2D P-SV elastic finite-difference framework for wave scattering in random
heterogeneous media, built from scratch in Python/NumPy.

- 4th-order staggered-grid solver with **convolutional PML**
- FFT-based **stochastic media** (von Kármán / Gaussian)
- **P/S energy separation** and coda analysis
- Independent **Monte Carlo radiative-transfer** model
- Energy conserved to within **0.6%**; CI-tested and reproducible

**Findings:** behind an 8 × 8 km anomaly, late coda energy rises **+43%** and S energy
**+54%**; the P→S energy ratio grows **~50×** as heterogeneity increases.
